John F. Kennedy

35th President of the United States

John Fitzgerald Kennedy was born on May 29, 1917 and died on November 22, 1963. He became the 35th President of the United States in 1961. Kennedy is remembered for his inspiring leadership and his famous quote: "Ask not what your country can do for you — ask what you can do for your country."

He played an important role during the Cuban Missile Crisis in 1962 and supported the Civil Rights Movement. His presidency gave hope to many Americans and made history during the Cold War.
