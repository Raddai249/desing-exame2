How to Make Coffee

Ingredients

  • 1 cup of water
  • 1 tablespoon of coffee powder
  • 1 teaspoon of sugar (optional)
  • Milk (optional)

Steps

  1. Boil the water in a kettle or pot.
  2. Add coffee powder into a cup.
  3. Pour hot water into the cup and stir well.
  4. Add sugar or milk if you want, then stir again.
  5. Your coffee is ready to drink!
